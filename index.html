Imitation learning · lerobot-il

This is a policy I trained. Drag the slider to change one inference-time setting and watch it go from failing to working.

The same checkpoint scores 0% or 34%, depending on how often it's allowed to look.

I trained an ACT policy on the Push-T benchmark and my first eval came back at 0% success. Before retraining anything I sat and watched the rollout videos. The policy was running a 100-step action chunk open-loop, so it overshot the goal and never looked again in time to correct. The weights were fine. The library's default execution horizon was what was wrong.

{% include pusht-figure.html %}

Fifty episodes is a small sample. At 34% the error bar is about ±6.5 points, so I can't say this beats my diffusion baseline at 30%. Every run here also stopped at 250 epochs, half the reference training budget. What the slider does show is that changing one inference-time setting took the same checkpoint from 0% to 34%.
