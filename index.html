Imitation learning · lerobot-il

The same checkpoint scores 0% or 34%, depending on how often it's allowed to look.

I trained an ACT policy on the Push-T benchmark and my first evaluation came back at 0.0% success. Instead of retraining, I watched the videos. The policy was committing to a 100-step action chunk open-loop — overshooting the goal and never re-observing in time to correct. The weights were fine. The library's default execution horizon wasn't.

{% include pusht-figure.html %}

What I won't claim: at n=50, binomial noise on a proportion near 0.3 is about ±6.5 points, so 34% vs. my diffusion baseline's 30% is suggestive, not proven — and everything here ran at 250 epochs, half the reference training budget. The defensible claim is the one on the slider: changing only the inference-time execution horizon moved the same checkpoint from 0% to 34%. The full write-up is in my notes.
